# JalSaarthi AI — 90-Second Hackathon Pitch

**Opening:**  
Every farmer faces one important question: *Should I irrigate today, or should I wait for rain?* A wrong decision can waste precious water, increase costs and put crops under stress.

**Problem:**  
Weather apps show forecasts, but farmers still need to translate rainfall and weather conditions into crop-specific irrigation decisions. Sensor-based solutions can also be costly or difficult to deploy at the beginning.

**Solution:**  
Introducing **JalSaarthi AI — Smarter Irrigation. Better Farming. Sustainable Water.** It is a sensor-free, weather-aware crop and water management assistant. A farmer enters the crop, soil, growth stage, farm area and village. JalSaarthi AI retrieves the local seven-day forecast and estimates crop-water demand, rainfall contribution and irrigation requirement.

**Live demo:**  
We select a crop, choose the soil and growth stage, enter the farm location and area, and generate a water plan. The dashboard shows rainfall, estimated crop demand, net irrigation, an indicative rainfall-deficit risk, daily recommendations and the approximate farm water volume. The farmer can download the plan. Marathi interface support makes the prototype more accessible.

**Differentiation:**  
Our first version does not require physical sensors. It is designed to be low-cost, explainable and extensible. We show the assumptions behind the calculations and plan to validate them with local agronomic data.

**Future vision:**  
Our roadmap includes locally validated ML models, optional IoT soil-moisture sensors, satellite insights, voice assistance and farmer profiles.

**Closing:**  
JalSaarthi AI aims to turn weather information into more informed irrigation decisions — helping move toward smarter and more sustainable water use.
